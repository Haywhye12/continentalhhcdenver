Communication & Swallowing Care

Speech & Language Therapy

Our licensed Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs) deliver evidence-based, in-home therapy for clients recovering from stroke, brain injury, neurological conditions, or surgery — helping them regain their voice, confidence, and quality of life.

Conditions We Address

  • Aphasia — difficulty speaking, reading, or writing after stroke
  • Dysarthria — slurred or weakened speech from neurological damage
  • Dysphagia — swallowing difficulties that risk aspiration
  • Cognitive-communication disorders (memory, attention, reasoning)
  • Voice disorders and vocal cord rehabilitation
  • Post-laryngectomy and tracheostomy communication support

Therapy Services Include

  • Individualized speech & language assessment and care planning
  • Articulation, fluency, and voice training exercises
  • Swallowing therapy and safe-eating strategies
  • Augmentative & Alternative Communication (AAC) device training
  • Cognitive rehabilitation — memory, problem-solving & attention
  • Family caregiver coaching and home exercise programs
  • Progress tracking and coordination with physicians & OT/PT teams
Request Speech Therapy

Who Benefits Most

  • Stroke and TBI survivors
  • Parkinson's disease patients
  • Post-cancer surgery (throat, mouth, neck)
  • ALS and multiple sclerosis clients
  • Seniors with age-related voice or swallowing changes
  • Adults with dementia-related communication decline

Why In-Home Speech Therapy?

  • Therapy in the real environment where challenges actually occur
  • No transportation stress for medically fragile clients
  • Family members can participate and reinforce exercises daily
  • Personalized pace — no rushed clinic schedules
  • Covered under Medicare, Medicaid & most insurance plans
