A Legacy of Care and Compassion

Enriching Lives Through Quality In-Home Healthcare

Continental Home Health, Inc. was founded in 2010 by a group of dedicated nurses and caregivers who shared a common vision: to provide exceptional in-home care that puts the client’s dignity and well-being first. We started with a small team and a handful of clients, driven by the belief that everyone deserves to receive professional, personalized care in the comfort and familiarity of their own home.

Over the years, our commitment to quality has allowed us to grow, but our core philosophy remains unchanged. We continue to be a family-owned and operated agency, treating every client like a member of our own family. Our team is our greatest asset, and their dedication is the reason we are able to provide the level of care that sets us apart.
